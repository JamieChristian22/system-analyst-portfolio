# Post-Go-Live Support Model

## Support Tiers

| Tier | Responsibility | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 Service Desk | Intake, known fixes, routing | Login guidance, browser issue, basic status |
| Tier 2 Application Support | Functional/application triage | Booking exceptions, notification retry, role issue |
| Tier 3 Engineering | Code/integration defects | API failures, concurrency bug, event-processing defect |
| Security/Privacy | Security and access escalation | Unauthorized access, suspicious activity |
| Vendor Support | External dependency issues | SMS provider, payment gateway |

---

## Hypercare

For the first 10 business days, the team conducts twice-daily health reviews covering booking success, interface failures, notification delivery, payment errors, latency, incidents, and customer/staff feedback.

---

## SLA Targets

| Priority | Initial Response | Update Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | 15 minutes | Every 30 minutes |
| P2 | 30 minutes | Every 2 hours |
| P3 | 4 business hours | Daily |
| P4 | 1 business day | As scheduled |
