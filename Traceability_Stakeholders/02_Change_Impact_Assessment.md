# Change Impact Assessment

## Impact Summary

| Area | Current State | Future State | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Patient Booking | Phone-first | Self-service + staff-assisted | High | Patient messaging, fallback staff workflow |
| Scheduler Workflow | Manual calendar checks | Unified console | High | Role-based training and quick guide |
| Reminders | Manual / inconsistent | Automated | Medium | Delivery monitoring and exception queue |
| Payments | Separate workflow | Integrated tokenized authorization | Medium | Revenue-cycle validation |
| Audit | Fragmented | Central event trail | Medium | Compliance training |
| Support | Ad hoc | Tiered support + monitoring | High | Runbooks, escalation matrix, hypercare |
| Reporting | Manual spreadsheet | Daily dashboard feed | Medium | KPI definitions and data validation |

---

## Change Risks

Key risks include staff adoption, patient digital-access variability, inaccurate provider schedules, dependency downtime, and support overload during launch. Mitigation is incorporated into training, phased rollout, monitoring, and hypercare.
